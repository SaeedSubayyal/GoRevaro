Common Questions

Questions we hear on most calls.

Can offshore reps actually sell to Western buyers?

It's a fair concern, and it's exactly why our vetting is built around that specific bar. Every candidate goes through a role-specific assessment evaluated by operators with 20+ years of combined experience managing GTM teams, testing fluency, confidence, objection handling, and the ability to hold a professional conversation with a discerning buyer. Candidates who don't clear that bar don't make the shortlist.

What happens if a rep underperforms after placement?

On Managed Teams, our Team Manager monitors performance on an ongoing basis, so underperformance is flagged early, not discovered at a quarterly review. If a rep isn't meeting agreed standards within the first 90 days, we replace them at no charge. After that, our Team Manager works the issue directly through coaching.

How quickly can you place a rep?

We typically present a shortlist of qualified candidates within 7 to 10 business days of receiving a brief. We maintain a warm pipeline of pre-vetted candidates specifically so we're never starting from zero.

Are you a competitor to recruitment agencies?

Not at all. Recruitment agencies help businesses fill seats. Go Revaro helps businesses build and run their revenue engine. We work exclusively across GTM functions, and many recruitment firms actually use us to build out their own BD function.

Can we start with one rep before committing to a full team?

Yes, and most clients do exactly that. Start with a single rep on either Direct Placement or Managed Teams. There's no minimum team size. See how it works, build confidence, and scale when you're ready.

How does the Custom AI Buildout work for our placed talent?

Before your new hire starts, our team builds custom AI workflows based on your ideal customer profile (ICP), product documentation, and sales collateral. We set up prompt libraries, research agents, and personalization systems, and train the candidate on these exact workflows prior to Day 1.

Do we need to purchase additional software or AI subscriptions?

No. Go Revaro provides the core AI prompts, custom research scripts, and workflow frameworks as part of our placement fee. We work inside the tools you already use, for example Salesforce, HubSpot, Apollo, Outreach, or LinkedIn Sales Navigator.
